Plate 14

Walnut sideboard

Tambour doors, brass pulls, tapered legs. This page is in three acts: scroll, and at each one the whole page (masthead, desk, every plate) turns to a new colour script, the way a film's does from act to act.

II

Twilight

Mountain sunset and lantern glow: data-act="wal-dusk"

The doors

Tambour, in quarter-sawn walnut

Nothing in this act is styled differently from the one above. Its section says data-act="wal-dusk", and when it reached the middle of the window the page retuned to Dusk: every surface, rule and ink re-derived from new rods, in one tween.

38 Slats per door

The brass pulls take the gold cue, so they change with the act too.

III

Firelight

Fern and campfire copper: data-act="wal-forest"

The legs

Tapered, turned by hand

Scroll back up and the acts play in reverse. Above the first, the page keeps the script it opened with. An act lasts until the next one begins, so a gap between acts never drops the page back.
